✅ RESIZE WINDOW:
   • Drag window edges
   • Drag window corners
   • Click maximize button
   • Double-click title bar

✅ FULLSCREEN MODE:
   • Press F11 to enter fullscreen
   • Press F11 again to exit
   • Press ESC to exit fullscreen

✅ MINIMUM SIZE:
   • Width: 800px
   • Height: 600px
   • Prevents too-small windows

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ HOW TO USE                                                               │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. START APPLICATION:
   mvn exec:java "-Dexec.mainClass=com.lfp.client.LFPClientApp"

2. RESIZE WINDOW:
   • Drag any edge or corner
   • Or click maximize button

3. FULLSCREEN:
   • Press F11 on any screen
   • Press F11 or ESC to exit

4. NAVIGATE:
   • All screens maintain resizable settings
   • F11 works on every screen
